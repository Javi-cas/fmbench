# Figure legends

**Do single-cell foundation models help predict knockdown responses in an unseen cell line?**
a, UMAP of control (non-targeting) cells from the seven cell lines (700 of the 2,000 per line shown), computed on the 6,360 genes measured in every line. b, Knockdown responses (log2 fold change versus each line's own controls) for the 16 strongest knockdowns shared by K562 (Replogle et al. 2022) and HCT116 (X-Atlas/Orion), on the 28 most variable genes; rows and columns clustered. The held-out line's responses (Jurkat or HepG2; Nadig et al. 2025) are never seen. c, Each line is represented by the mean of its control-cell vectors: the raw expression profile (R0) or a foundation-model embedding (R1, scGPT whole-human; R2, Geneformer V2-104M; R3, State SE-600M). d, The held-out line's responses are predicted as an average of the five source lines' responses, weighted by the softmax of the centred cosine similarity between line vectors; the temperature T is chosen by leave-one-source-out on the sources only. U, equal weights. Weights shown are illustrative. e, Pre-registered readout: a foundation model helps only if its gain over R0 (pooled Spearman correlation of gene-centred knockdown-specific effects, target gene masked; paired bootstrap over knockdowns; Bonferroni-adjusted 98.3% CI) excludes zero on both held-out lines.

**Can Boltz-2 tell which metabolites regulate which human enzymes?**
a, Benchmark design: 12 established enzyme-regulator pairs across 10 metabolic enzymes (red) and the same six decoy metabolites for every enzyme (grey): two phosphorylated (3-phosphoglycerate, glycerol-3-phosphate), two sugars (sucrose, trehalose) and two small metabolites (succinate, taurine). Asterisks mark regulatory sites at subunit interfaces, which single-chain inputs cannot form. b, Boltz-2 prediction of AMPK γ1 (PRKAG1) with AMP from the benchmark's test run, coloured by per-residue confidence (pLDDT); secondary structure assigned from Cα geometry. Dashed box, the AMP pocket, enlarged below. c, Per-pair score: the share of the six decoys the regulator outranks on Boltz-2's binder probability (schematic; chance 0.5). d, Aggregate test: mean score over the 12 pairs against a uniform-rank null (100,000 draws); a mean of at least 0.65 is needed for one-sided P < 0.05. Predicted affinity is the secondary score; the phosphorylated decoys alone, and within-chain versus interface sites, are reported separately.
