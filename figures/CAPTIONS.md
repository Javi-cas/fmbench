# Figure legends

**Do single-cell foundation models help predict knockdown responses in an unseen cell line?**
a, Data. Top, UMAP of control (non-targeting) cells from the seven cell lines (700 of the 2,000 per line shown), on the 6,360 genes measured in every line. Bottom, knockdown responses (log2 fold change versus each line's own controls) for the 16 strongest knockdowns shared by K562 (Replogle et al. 2022) and HCT116 (X-Atlas/Orion), on the 28 most variable genes, rows and columns clustered; the held-out line's responses (Jurkat or HepG2; Nadig et al. 2025) are never seen. b, Representation. Each line is the mean of its 2,000 control-cell vectors: the raw expression profile (R0) or a foundation-model embedding (R1, scGPT whole-human; R2, Geneformer V2-104M; R3, State SE-600M), with the same cells and genes for every arm. c, Prediction. The held-out line's responses are an average of the five source lines' responses weighted by the softmax of the centred cosine similarity between line vectors; T is chosen by leave-one-source-out on the sources only. U, equal weights. Weights shown are illustrative. d, Evaluation. Pre-registered tests: H1, a foundation model beats R0 (pooled Spearman correlation of gene-centred knockdown-specific effects, target gene masked; paired bootstrap; Bonferroni-adjusted 98.3% CI) on both held-out lines; H2, it beats plain pooling; every arm must beat its own shuffled labels.

**Can Boltz-2 tell which metabolites regulate which human enzymes?**
a, Benchmark. 12 established enzyme-regulator pairs across 10 metabolic enzymes (red) and the same six decoys for every enzyme (grey): two phosphorylated (3-phosphoglycerate, glycerol-3-phosphate), two sugars (sucrose, trehalose) and two small metabolites (succinate, taurine). Asterisks, regulatory sites between subunits. b, Co-folding. Sequence, ColabFold alignment and ligand go into Boltz-2, which returns a complex, a binder probability and an affinity. Shown: the prediction for AMPK γ1 (PRKAG1) with AMP from the test run, coloured by per-residue confidence (pLDDT), secondary structure assigned from Cα geometry; dashed box, the AMP pocket enlarged below. c, Ranking. Each pair is scored by the share of its six decoys the regulator outranks on binder probability (top, schematic; chance 0.5); the mean over the 12 pairs is compared with a uniform-rank null (bottom, 100,000 draws), where a mean of at least 0.65 gives one-sided P < 0.05. d, Readouts reported separately: all decoys; phosphorylated decoys only; interface versus within-chain sites. Most complexes have pre-2023 PDB structures, so the benchmark tests recall of known biology, not discovery.
